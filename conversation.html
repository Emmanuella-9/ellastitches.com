CONNECTING WITH INFLUENCERS: THE WHY AND HOW

Isaac Newton famously said, “If I have seen further, it is by standing on the shoulders of giants.” This quote reminds us how mentors, connectors and influencers can help us go farther, faster. Sometimes, it’s not just about what you know — it’s about who knows you. In life, there are places you cannot get to on your own; you need someone to introduce you. If you’ve been diligent and consistent but haven’t received the rewards you deserve, it may be because you haven’t been discovered by the right people. Your potential blossoms when your gifts gain the attention of influencers and authority figures. Instead of resenting successful people, understand the connection principle and learn how to use it in your favour.
