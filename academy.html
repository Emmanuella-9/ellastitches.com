FASHION ACADEMY

MAKE YOUR DREAM A REALITY.

BRING YOUR CREATIVITY TO LIFE!

Let’s help you hone your skills and express your creativity.

At Ella stitches and style Fashion Academy, we are known for our dedication to excellence and the creativity that permeates all aspects of our work.

Our comprehensive training programs are designed to transform you from beginner to expert with a solid foundation in the theory and practice of fashion design even if you’ve never used needle and thread before. Our curriculum covers all you need to know about sewing tools and equipment, tailoring terms and definitions, sewing techniques, pattern drafting, fabric types, garment construction, and much more.

We also teach the business side of fashion so that you can understand how to profit from your skills. Sounds great! Doesn’t it? If you’ve ever desired the kind of complete training that will help you to stand out as an expert fashion designer, then your search stops at Ella stitiches and styles Fashion Academy.

MEET THE LEAD TUTOR.

Emmanuella Ebubechukwu is the Creative Director of Ella stitches and styles as well as the head tutor at Ella stitches and styles Academy. She is a passionate fashion entrepreneur who is committed to expressing creativity with a keen sense of excellence. Her proactive spirit and determined disposition are two of the entrepreneurial qualities that have helped her rise above apparent limitations to birth Ella stitches and styles. she is an alumna of Imo state university where she got her BSc in Accountancy. she is driven by her passion for fashion as a vehicle for helping young people to not only express their creativity but also overcome the challenge of unemployment, thus empowering them to rise above seemingly impossible situations.
