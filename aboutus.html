About Us

Ella Stitches and Styles, we bring creativity, elegance, and precision together to create designs that speak confidence and individuality. As a passionate fashion designer, our goal is to craft outfits that fit beautifully, feel comfortable, and reflect your unique style.

From custom-made pieces to detailed alterations, every stitch is handled with care, quality, and attention to detail. We believe fashion is more than clothing—it’s a way to express who you are.

At Ella Stitches and Styles, your style is our priority, and excellence is our standard.

More About Us

Ella Stitches and styles is a Nigerian fashion design brand that started full operation in 2022.

We specialise in designing and making bespoke and ready-to-wear outfits for the classy and modest contemporary woman.

We also design and produce kids clothing as well as hand-made fashion accessories. We have a reputation for delivering neatly sewn and well-tailored garments in a timely fashion.

In addition to our tailoring and fashion design services, we have a well-structured training system designed to empower young people and mentor them in establishing and building their own businesses.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be a brand that provides quality clothing that meet local demands while also competing favourably with foreign fashion brands.

We seek to create clothes that combine simplicity with elegance, all within a practical/realistic budget.
