PRACTICAL TIPS FOR GETTING STARTED WITH PATTERN DRAFTING

Pattern drafting can be overwhelming for beginners, especially if you don’t know where to start. Don’t worry about getting it perfect yet; what’s really important is to actually get started! Just like learning any other skill, perfection comes with consistent practice over time. From learning how to make a dress pattern to drafting necklines, these tips will help you get started. With a little help and some expert tricks, you’ll soon design pieces you once only dreamed of!

1. GET THE RIGHT TOOLS.

Just like cooking, it’s hard to do the right job without the right tool. Some tools can be substituted, but many have a singular, important task that only they can achieve. Patternmaking tools are critical — it’s worth investing in quality items. Pictured above are key tools: clear ruler, small 5" scissors, rotary cutter, seam allowance marker, mechanical pencil, and a French curve. Additionally, use pattern paper and manila oak tag paper for drafting and transferring patterns.
